Add FramperBot

Bring FramperBot to your channel

FramperBot joins your chat and turns commands and channel point rewards into overlays: rain, Pokémon sprites, a background pattern, a character randomizer and a chat with Mystery Dungeon portraits. It's free.

  • The bot joins your chat and answers its own commands.
  • FramperBot reads your channel point rewards and redemptions (nothing else on your account).
  • You can remove it at any time here, which deletes your channel's data.

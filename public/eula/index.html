2. What FramperBot does

FramperBot turns Twitch chat and channel point rewards into stream overlays: rain, Pokémon sprites, a background color and pattern, a character randomizer for several games, and a chat overlay with Pokémon Mystery Dungeon style portraits. It consists of this website, OBS browser sources, a chat bot and a dashboard.

5. Adding FramperBot and the chat bot

Only the owner of a Twitch channel can add FramperBot to it, by logging in with Twitch on the dashboard. By adding it, the Broadcaster agrees that our chat bot joins their channel's chat to read and answer the App's commands and to show chat on the Broadcaster's PMD chat overlay, and that FramperBot receives the channel's channel point redemptions from Twitch. The bot only acts on its own commands and rewards, and does not store other chat messages.

We may limit how many channels FramperBot joins.

6. Commands and features

  • !dealwithit [game] [role] [@user]: rolls a random character. Each viewer can use it once every 3 minutes; the Broadcaster is exempt.
  • !portrait <dex number> [emotion]: picks the portrait shown next to your messages on the PMD chat overlay.
  • !rainbearer: shows who last summoned the rain.
  • !pmdconfig and !cardstyle: Broadcaster-only settings, also available on the dashboard.
  • Channel point rewards the Broadcaster links on the dashboard: rain, sprite, background color, background pattern and randomizer reroll.

Features may change over time. Channel points are Twitch's: we don't control redemptions, refunds or point balances.

Chat-powered overlays for Pokémon streams

Let chat run the show.

FramperBot listens to Twitch chat and channel points, and the stream reacts: rain rolls in, Pokémon pop up around the frame, the background changes, and every message gets a Mystery Dungeon portrait.

    How it works

    1. 1

      Viewers chat or redeem

      A channel point reward or a command like !dealwithit, right in Twitch chat.

    2. 2

      FramperBot picks it up

      The bot checks it, answers in chat, and tells that channel's overlays what to show.

    3. 3

      The stream reacts

      OBS browser sources play it live: rain, sprites, a new background or a UNITE roll.

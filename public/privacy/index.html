1. Who is responsible

The controller of your data is Fernando E. ("Frampersand"), an individual based in Chile. Contact: frampersand@gmail.com.

2. What we process

Broadcasters (streamers who add FramperBot to their channel)

  • Twitch user ID and login name, received when you log in with Twitch.
  • The permission you granted: reading your channel point rewards and redemptions (channel:read:redemptions), and the status of the resulting Twitch EventSub subscription.
  • A copy of your custom channel point rewards (ID, title, cost, prompt, whether they ask for text, whether they're on), read when you log in, so you can link them on the dashboard.
  • Your settings: linked rewards, randomizer card style and PMD chat look.
  • Which version of the EULA and this policy you accepted, and when.

We never see your Twitch password or email address. The login token Twitch gives us is used once, to read your reward list, and is not stored; it expires on its own.

Viewers (people chatting or redeeming rewards in a channel with FramperBot)

  • Chat messages: our bot reads chat in those channels to recognise its commands (such as !dealwithit), and passes messages, with your display name and name color, to that channel's PMD chat overlay so they appear on stream. Ordinary messages are not stored or logged.
  • Channel point redemptions: Twitch sends us your login, display name, the reward and any text you entered, so we can run the effect. A one-line record of each redemption is kept in the server's logs.
  • Saved choices: if you use !portrait, we keep your display name, the portrait you picked and the channel. If you summon the rain, your display name is kept as that channel's Rainbearer.
  • In memory only: command cooldowns and your last randomizer game, discarded when the server restarts.

Everyone

  • Your IP address and basic request data, processed by our hosting provider and Cloudflare to deliver and protect the website and overlays.
  • Server logs containing channel names, commands, redemptions and errors.

3. Why, and on what legal basis

  • Providing FramperBot to Broadcasters (login, settings, rewards, overlays): performance of our agreement with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Processing viewer chat, commands and redemptions: our legitimate interest in running the interactive features the Broadcaster has enabled (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Logs, security and abuse prevention: our legitimate interest in keeping FramperBot working and secure (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Recording EULA and policy acceptance: our legitimate interest in being able to show what was agreed (Art. 6(1)(f)).

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

4. Who receives data

  • Twitch Interactive, Inc.: login, the Twitch API, EventSub and chat run on Twitch.
  • DigitalOcean, LLC: hosts the server (a DigitalOcean Droplet in the United States) and stores the data listed above.
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: network and security provider in front of the website, the overlays and our image server.
  • Image and font hosts: some overlays and pages load images or fonts from other sites, which then see the viewing device's IP address: pokemondb.net and projectpokemon.org (animated sprites), unite.pokemon.com (a card background) and Google Fonts (on the dashboard and the randomizer). In practice that device is usually the Broadcaster's OBS.

5. International transfers

Our server is located in the United States, and Twitch, Cloudflare and DigitalOcean are United States companies, so your data is stored and processed in the United States. Transferring it there is necessary to provide FramperBot. Where data leaves the EU/EEA or the UK, these transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, which DigitalOcean includes in its Data Processing Agreement.

6. How long we keep data

  • Broadcaster account, accepted terms, settings, reward links and reward list: until FramperBot is removed from the channel. Logging in again replaces the reward list.
  • Viewers' portrait choices and the Rainbearer: until changed, or until FramperBot is removed from the channel.
  • Login token: not kept.
  • Cooldowns and last randomizer game: until the server restarts.
  • Server logs: until the server is next updated, which happens often.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Chile's Law 19.628 as amended by Law 21.719, or the EU or UK GDPR), you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, ask us to restrict processing, receive your data in a portable format, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Email frampersand@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

Broadcasters can remove FramperBot from their channel at any time on the dashboard. This deletes the channel's settings, reward links, reward list, viewers' portrait choices, the Rainbearer and the Twitch connection straight away. You can also disconnect FramperBot under Connections in your Twitch settings.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority: in Chile, the Agencia de Protección de Datos Personales, or the authority in the country where you live.

8. Cookies and local storage

The dashboard sets two cookies, both strictly necessary for logging in: fb_oauth_state (ten minutes, protects the Twitch login from forgery) and fb_session (seven days, keeps you logged in). We use no analytics or trackers. The showcase remembers its rain volume in your browser's local storage; that stays on your device.

9. Children

FramperBot is meant for people who meet Twitch's minimum age in their country and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly process data of children below that age. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We will post changes here with a new version and effective date. If changes are significant, we will ask Broadcasters to accept the new version on the dashboard.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: frampersand@gmail.com
